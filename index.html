Who We Are

The Voice of Telugu NRIs in Germany

TDP Germany is the official overseas chapter of the Telugu Desam Party, founded on the values of self-respect, progress, and welfare. We bring together Telugu professionals, students, and families settled across Germany to stay connected with our roots and contribute to the growth of Andhra Pradesh.

As part of the global NRI TDP Cell, we serve as a bridge between the diaspora and the homeland — channeling ideas, investment, and support toward a Swarna Andhra Pradesh.

  • Community networking and cultural unity
  • Policy awareness and civic engagement
  • Support for back-home development initiatives
