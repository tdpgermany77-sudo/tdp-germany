Who We Are

The Voice of Telugu NRIs in Germany

TDP Germany is the overseas family of the Telugu Desam Party — built on self-respect, togetherness, and care for one another. We bring together Telugu professionals, students, and families across Germany so that no one feels alone, far from home.

As part of the global NRI TDP Cell, we are a bridge between our community here and our homeland — sharing ideas, opportunities, and a helping hand for the growth and well-being of Telugu people. Guided by Sri N. Chandrababu Naidu and Sri Nara Lokesh, we work to lift each other up.

  • A warm community that celebrates our festivals and culture together
  • A helping hand for students and families settling into life in Germany
  • Support in times of need — for every Telugu's growth and success

Our Pillars

Founding Members

Three like-minded Telugus in Germany first came together at a protest for Special Category Status for Andhra Pradesh. United by that cause, they saw the need for a strong Telugu Desam voice abroad and established the NRI TDP Germany wing — to raise our community's issues, support our people here, and stand with our homeland.
